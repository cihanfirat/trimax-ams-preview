FAQ

Questions association boards ask us.

What is an association management company?

An association management company (AMC) provides the staff, systems and expertise to run a member organization's daily operations, so the volunteer board can focus on direction and members instead of logistics.

How much does association management cost?

Pricing depends on your size, event calendar and which services you need. After a short discovery call, we send a clear proposal with a fixed monthly scope so your board can budget with confidence.

Can we start with just one service?

Yes. Many organizations start with events, finances or the website and add more over time. You choose the scope.

How does onboarding work?

We document your current processes, take over systems and accounts in a planned order, and meet with your board regularly during the transition so nothing is dropped.

Who will we work with day to day?

A named account manager who knows your organization, supported by specialists in events, finance and technology.
